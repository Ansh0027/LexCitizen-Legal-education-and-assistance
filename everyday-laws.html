Laws for ordinary moments

08 guides
Consumer Protection Act, 2019

When a purchase goes wrong

Consumers can seek repair, replacement, refund, or compensation when goods or services are defective, deficient, unfair, or misleading.

Start here

  • Keep invoices, screenshots, warranties, and complaint numbers.
  • Complain to the seller first, then use the consumer commission process.
Limit: A consumer complaint is not a shortcut for every commercial or employment dispute.
Right to Information Act, 2005

Ask a public authority

Citizens can request information held by public authorities, subject to exemptions such as national security, privacy, and privileged material.

Start here

  • Ask for specific records, dates, decisions, or documents.
  • Use the First Appeal route if there is no response or the reply is incomplete.
Limit: RTI seeks existing information; it does not require an authority to create an answer or explain every policy choice.
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Your personal data

The law creates duties for data fiduciaries and rights for individuals around notice, consent, access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal.

Start here

  • Read the notice and use the platform's grievance channel.
  • Share only data needed for a clear purpose.
Limit: Rights and obligations operate within the Act's notified framework, exemptions, and rules.
Information Technology Act, 2000

Safety and responsibility online

Cyber offences, intermediary responsibilities, electronic records, and certain digital conduct are addressed through the IT framework and related rules.

Start here

  • Preserve URLs, messages, headers, transaction records, and timestamps.
  • Report financial cyber fraud quickly through official channels.
Limit: Not every unpleasant, critical, or offensive post is a crime; the exact facts and current law matter.
Renting and housing

Know your tenancy paperwork

Rental rights vary by state law and by the agreement, but written terms help clarify rent, deposit, repairs, notice, and possession.

Start here

  • Use a written agreement and record the condition of the property.
  • Document rent payments, notices, repairs, and communication.
Limit: State rent-control and tenancy rules differ. A general guide cannot replace local law.
Workplace protections

Fairness at work

Employment agreements, wage rules, social security laws, and protections against sexual harassment shape the workplace relationship.

Start here

  • Keep the offer letter, payslips, policies, and written complaints.
  • Use the Internal Committee process for workplace sexual harassment complaints where applicable.
Limit: The correct forum and limitation period depend on the issue, employer, and applicable labour law.

Before you act

Make a small evidence file.

  • Write down dates, names, and what happened.
  • Save original documents and clear copies.
  • Communicate in writing where possible.
  • Check the deadline for a complaint or appeal.

Important note

General information is a starting point.

Laws change, and facts decide outcomes. For a serious dispute, seek advice from a qualified lawyer or an official legal-aid service.
